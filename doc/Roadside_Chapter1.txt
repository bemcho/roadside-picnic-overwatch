
Route 1: Chapter 1 (The "Full Empty" Run with Kirill)

In this expedition, Redrick works officially as a lab assistant for scientist Kirill Panov, taking a motorized "boot" (an all-terrain hover-vehicle) into the Zone legally through an Institute checkpoint.
1. The International Institute (Starting Point): Redrick and Kirill suit up in protective gear at the Institute on the border of Harmont.
2. The Institute Checkpoint / Perimeter Gate: They drive the "boot" through the official UN/Institute security gate into the Zone.
3. The Broken Asphalt Highway: They follow the cracked, abandoned main road heading straight into the outer Zone.
4. The Old Factory / Industrial Sector: Passing the ruined factory buildings on their flank, they navigate around visible distortion zones ("mosquito balds").
5. The Garage (Target Destination): They reach the ruined transport garage—the exact spot where Redrick previously spotted a rare artifact ("full empty").
6. The Return Path (Back to the Institute): After securing the artifact and narrowly avoiding a "silver cobweb" inside the garage, they retrace their route back along the highway to the Institute checkpoint.
