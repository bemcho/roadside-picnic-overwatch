For a 3D game, you will want to translate the novel's literary concepts into concrete physics behaviors, shaders, audio cues, and inventory systems.

1. Core 3D Gameplay Loop & Player Systems

• Physics-Based Probing (Nuts & Bolts): Give the player an infinite or craftable pouch of metal nuts tied to cloth strips (for visibility). Throwing them triggers real-time physics interactions—sudden downward acceleration, mid-air suspension, or violent twisting—revealing invisible anomalies.
• Sensory Detection (No HUD Radars): Avoid standard mini-maps. Use diegetic cues:
	• Audio: Subtle low-frequency hums, static crackles, or sudden dead silence near anomalies.
	• Visual Shaders: Slight chromatic aberration, heat-haze refraction, or shadows pointing toward the light source instead of away.
• Stealth & Perimeter Crossing: Implement a day/night cycle with UN perimeter patrols, searchlights, automated acoustic/motion sensors, and mud-bog terrain that slows movement and increases stamina drain.
• Diegetic Containment & Backpack Weight: High-tier artifacts require specialized containers (e.g., ceramic jars for Hell Slime, lead-lined cases for Death Lamps). Carrying unshielded artifacts applies continuous AoE debuffs to the player and nearby AI.

4. Meta-Progression & World Systems

• Factions & Economy:
	• The Institute: Pays reliably for intact artifacts and scientific readings, unlocking better suits, detectors, and containment flasks.
	• Black Market (Borscht / Bar): Pays higher cash for illegal/dangerous items (like Hell Slime or Itchers) and sells untraceable gear, bribes, and forged passes.
• Contamination & Long-Term Consequences: Instead of standard radiation that clears with a pill, track cumulative Zone Exposure. High exposure triggers hallucinations, attracts anomalies, and unlocks darker narrative endings rather than just lowering HP.
