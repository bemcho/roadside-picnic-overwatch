
Route 2: Chapter 4 (The Final Expedition to the Golden Sphere)

Years later, Redrick undertakes an illegal, on-foot trek with Arthur Burbridge (the Vulture's son) to reach the deepest point of the Zone.
1. The Borscht / Edge of Harmont (Staging): Redrick and Arthur prepare in town before sneaking out under cover of darkness.
2. The Unattended Perimeter Breach: Instead of using the Institute gate, they slip through a gap in the barbed wire and concrete cordon near the Plague Quarter / Old Suburbs, evading UN searchlights and patrols.
3. The Ditch & Abandoned Suburbs: They crawl through drainage ditches and ruined residential yards on the outer rim to stay out of sight of border guards.
4. The Rail Line / Embankment: Moving deeper, they follow an overgrown, rusted railway embankment that acts as a corridor past the early anomaly fields.
5. The Marsh / Swamp: They cross the treacherous wetland interior, tossing metal nuts ahead of every step to detect invisible gravity traps ("mosquito balds") and skirting around pools of lethal "witches' jelly."
6. The Hills / Steep Ridge: Emerging from the marsh, they climb up a rocky, barren ridge that overlooks the innermost basin of the Zone.
7. The Quarry Rim & The "Meat Grinder": Descending toward the massive excavation pit, they reach the narrow entrance path guarded by the invisible, lethal "Meat Grinder" anomaly.
8. The Golden Sphere (Final Destination): After Arthur walks ahead and triggers the Meat Grinder, Redrick crosses the cleared path down into the floor of the Quarry, reaching the Golden Sphere.
